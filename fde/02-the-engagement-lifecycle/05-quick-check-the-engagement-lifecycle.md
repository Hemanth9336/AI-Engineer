### Q1. Put the stages in order.

- A. Pilot, discovery, handoff, production
- B. Discovery, production, pilot, handoff
- C. Discovery, pilot, production, handoff
- D. Production, pilot, discovery, handoff

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: C** - Learn, prove with a few users, harden for everyone, hand over.

</details>

### Q2. Which is the strongest pilot exit criterion?

- A. At least 85% correct on the 60-question test set and 100% refusal on out-of-scope questions
- B. The assistant feels accurate
- C. Users are happy
- D. The executive liked the demo

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: A** - Measurable criteria can be checked by anyone.

</details>

### Q3. Which work belongs mainly to the production stage?

- A. Interviewing users
- B. Cost limits, logging, guardrails and security approval
- C. Choosing the use case
- D. Writing the scoping document

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: B** - Production makes a proven pilot safe for everyone.

</details>

### Q4. When does handoff preparation start?

- A. On the last day
- B. Never - the FDE keeps running it
- C. After the contract ends
- D. During the pilot - the future owner works alongside you

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: D** - Training the owner early is what makes handoff work.

</details>
