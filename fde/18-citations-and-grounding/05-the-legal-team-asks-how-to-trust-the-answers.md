Kestrel Bank's legal counsel joins the pilot review: "Your assistant answers staff questions about lending policy. If it states a wrong rule and someone acts on it, we're exposed. How do we know any answer is right?"

### Q1. What is the strongest first answer?

- A. Every answer cites the policy sections it used, so staff can check the source in one click, and uncited answers are blocked
- B. The model is very accurate
- C. We use the newest model
- D. Staff should double-check everything manually

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: A** - Citations make each answer verifiable.

</details>

### Q2. Counsel asks: 'And what happens when the policy doesn't cover the question?'

- A. It replies with a fixed refusal and offers a route to the policy team; we test this with trap questions every release
- B. The model gives its best guess
- C. It searches the internet
- D. It asks the user to rephrase until it can answer

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: A** - A tested, exact refusal is the safe failure.

</details>

### Q3. What evidence should you bring to the next review?

- A. A live demo
- B. Eval results: answer accuracy, citation validity and refusal rate on trap questions, plus examples of failures and fixes
- C. The model's benchmark scores
- D. A list of features

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: B** - Measured results on the bank's own questions.

</details>
