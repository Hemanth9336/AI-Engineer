### Q1. Why include a trap question in a demo?

- A. It shows the refusal behaviour - the safety property customers most need to see
- B. To trick the customer
- C. To make it longer
- D. It's required

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: A** - Show the system being honest.

</details>

### Q2. An answer is wrong during the demo. Best reaction?

- A. Blame the model provider
- B. Restart the demo
- C. Acknowledge it, open the sources, explain the failure mode and how it's fixed and tested
- D. Ignore it

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: C** - Calm investigation builds trust.

</details>

### Q3. Which is a well-stated accuracy claim?

- A. 'It's basically always right'
- B. 'Better than ChatGPT'
- C. 'State of the art'
- D. '47 of 50 golden questions correct, graded by your team; all 10 traps refused'

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: D** - Specific, measured, verifiable.

</details>

### Q4. What belongs in the statement of work instead of '100% accuracy'?

- A. Nothing about quality
- B. An agreed eval set, a measurable bar, reporting cadence and a fix-and-retest process
- C. A bigger model
- D. A longer pilot

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: B** - Measured commitments.

</details>
