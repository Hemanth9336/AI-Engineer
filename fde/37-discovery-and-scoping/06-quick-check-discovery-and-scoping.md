### Q1. What makes a strong first AI use case?

- A. High volume, answers in existing documents, a measurable outcome, and a cheap mistake
- B. The most exciting idea
- C. Whatever the CEO likes
- D. The hardest problem

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: A** - Prove value fast where risk is low.

</details>

### Q2. 'The knowledge is in Pieter's head.' What does that signal?

- A. A great use case
- B. A fine-tuning project
- C. A need for a bigger model
- D. A bad fit for now - there's no data to retrieve or evaluate

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: D** - No data, no RAG.

</details>

### Q3. Why write an out-of-scope list?

- A. Lawyers require it
- B. It makes decisions durable and stops rejected ideas from returning every meeting
- C. It's shorter
- D. To blame others later

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: B** - Written scope protects the project.

</details>

### Q4. Which is a good success metric?

- A. 'Better support'
- B. 'Use AI'
- C. 'First reply time for repeat questions from 2.5 days to under 4 hours'
- D. 'Happy users'

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: C** - Specific, measurable, with a baseline.

</details>
