### Q1. What makes an engineer "forward deployed"?

- A. They work on the newest models
- B. They build the solution inside one customer's world - their systems, data and people
- C. They only do demos
- D. They work from the vendor's headquarters

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: B** - Forward deployed means you go to the customer and build there.

</details>

### Q2. Which of these is NOT one of the four FDE responsibilities?

- A. Find the right problem
- B. Prove it works
- C. Train a new foundation model
- D. Make it survive

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: C** - Training models is ML engineering. The FDE finds, builds, proves and hardens.

</details>

### Q3. In the day-in-the-life lesson, why does the FDE let Priya write the code even though it is slower?

- A. Priya types faster
- B. She will own the system after the FDE leaves - teaching her is part of making it survive
- C. The FDE is not allowed to code
- D. It looks better to the customer

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: B** - Handoff starts long before the last day.

</details>

### Q4. A support lead finds a trick question the assistant gets wrong. What is the best first move?

- A. Hide it before Friday's review
- B. Write it down and add it to the test set
- C. Switch to a bigger model immediately
- D. Tell the lead not to ask trick questions

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: B** - Real failures become test cases - that list is how quality improves and how you prove it.

</details>
