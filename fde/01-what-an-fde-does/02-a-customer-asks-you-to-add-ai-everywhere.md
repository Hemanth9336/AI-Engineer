### Q1. You arrive at a retail company for your first week of an engagement. In the kickoff meeting the VP of Operations says, "We bought AI access, so let's add AI to everything - the warehouse system, the HR portal, the finance reports, all of it. Can you start this week?" The room looks at you. What do you say?

- A. Suggest picking one painful, measurable problem first, and offer to run short interviews with the teams this week to find it
- B. Ask the VP to write a requirements document listing every desired AI feature, and wait for it before starting
- C. Agree, and start wiring the model into all four systems in parallel so everyone sees activity
- D. Tell the VP that most of these systems do not need AI and the request shows they do not understand the technology

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: A** - Finding the right problem is the first responsibility. One painful, measurable use case gives you something you can build fast and prove. Waiting for a document stalls the engagement, spreading across four systems guarantees nothing ships, and lecturing the VP loses the room.

</details>
