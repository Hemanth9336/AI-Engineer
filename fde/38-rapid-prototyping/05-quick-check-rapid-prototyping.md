### Q1. What question does a PoC answer?

- A. Is it production-ready?
- B. What should it cost?
- C. Will it scale to millions?
- D. Can the core idea work on the customer's real data?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: D** - Feasibility, quickly.

</details>

### Q2. Which is fine to skip in a PoC?

- A. A polished UI
- B. Citations
- C. Refusals
- D. Keeping keys out of code

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: A** - Polish comes later.

</details>

### Q3. Why use the customer's real data even in a PoC?

- A. It's required by law
- B. It's faster
- C. Public data proves nothing about their documents and questions - and their messy data is what you need to learn about
- D. Models need it

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: C** - Real data reveals real problems.

</details>

### Q4. What should you expect to do with PoC code?

- A. Ship it
- B. Probably rewrite it - keep the learnings, eval questions and notes
- C. Delete everything
- D. Patent it

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: B** - The insights outlive the code.

</details>
