The PoC demo goes well. Marta Kovacs says: "This is great - can we just put it on the supplier portal next week? It already works." Your PoC has no redaction, no logging, no eval set beyond ten questions, and runs from your laptop.

### Q1. What's the right response?

- A. 'Sure, next week'
- B. Quietly ship it
- C. 'No, it's not ready' with no plan
- D. Thank her, then walk through the 'needed before production' list - evals, redaction, monitoring, integration - with a realistic plan to get there, including a staged pilot

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: D** - Turn excitement into a plan, not a refusal.

</details>

### Q2. Which missing item is the strongest reason not to put it on the public portal next week?

- A. No dark mode
- B. Code style
- C. No golden-set evaluation, so nobody knows how often it's wrong on real supplier questions - and suppliers would act on its answers with no human check
- D. The model's name

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: C** - Unmeasured quality, no human in the loop.

</details>

### Q3. What faster step can you offer instead?

- A. A pilot with the six desk agents using drafts they review before sending, while the eval set and redaction are built
- B. Nothing
- C. Ship to all suppliers but add a disclaimer
- D. A second PoC

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer: A** - Internal users with review are the safe first audience.

</details>
